# Nativx Assistant - Brand & Go-To-Market Kit

Document de lucru pentru branding, pozitionare, copy, identitate vizuala si materiale necesare lansarii Nativx Assistant.

Surse interne folosite:
- `LANDING-PREMIUM-PACK.md`
- `README.md`
- `docs/ARCHITECTURE-AUDIT.md`

Principiu de baza: brandul trebuie sa transmita incredere operationala, nu magie AI. Nativx Assistant nu este "inca un chatbot". Este un asistent de vanzari AI, gestionat de Nativx, care recomanda produse, recupereaza cosuri si raspunde clientilor pe canale conversationale, fara sa inventeze preturi sau linkuri.

**Nativx Assistant**

Utilizare recomandata:
- Prima mentiune: `Nativx Assistant`

### Descriere scurta

**Nativx Assistant este asistentul de vanzari AI pentru magazine online, instalat si rulat de Nativx pe website, WhatsApp si canale de test precum Telegram. Recomanda produse, raspunde clientilor, recupereaza cosuri si verifica fiecare pret si link in catalogul real al magazinului.**

### Descriere intr-o fraza

**The AI sales assistant for online stores - set up and run by Nativx.**

Nativx Assistant ajuta magazinele online sa transforme conversatiile in vanzari. ## 2. Pozitionare

### Pozitionarea centrala

**Nativx Assistant este asistentul de vanzari AI pe care magazinele mari il construiesc intern, livrat ca serviciu managed pentru magazine online care nu au echipa AI proprie.**

### Diferentiatorul principal

**Catalog-accurate by design.**

Nativx Assistant nu trebuie sa para "mai creativ" decat alte AI-uri. Trebuie sa para mai sigur, mai util si mai vandabil. Diferentiatorul real este faptul ca preturile, produsele si linkurile sunt verificate in catalogul real inainte ca mesajul sa fie trimis.

### Ce promitem

Promisiuni permise:
- recomanda produse din catalogul real
- raspunde in romana, maghiara si engleza, unde configuratia permite
- pastreaza istoricul clientului intre canale, daca identitatea este legata corect
- verifica preturi si linkuri inainte de trimitere
- poate escalada catre om
- poate rula ca serviciu managed
- poate fi testat inainte de go-live

Promisiuni de evitat pana exista dovada:
- procente de crestere a vanzarilor
- timpi exacti de raspuns
- ROI garantat
- testimoniale inventate
- "live in 24/48h" daca nu este angajament contractual
- "integrare nativa Shopify/Woo/Magento/PrestaShop" daca nu exista connector nativ
- "Telegram productie" cat timp este canal de test
- "EU-hosted" sau "data residency EU" pana este verificat contractual

### Brand promise

**Every chat can become a sale, but every answer must stay grounded in your real catalog.**

Varianta in romana:

**Fiecare conversatie poate deveni o vanzare, dar fiecare raspuns ramane ancorat in catalogul real al magazinului.**

### Tagline recomandat

Primary:
- **The AI sales assistant for online stores.**

Alternative:
- **Your best salesperson, on every channel.**
- **Sales conversations grounded in your real catalog.**
- **The managed AI sales assistant for ecommerce.**
- **One assistant. Every channel. Real catalog data.**

Tagline romana:
- **Asistentul AI de vanzari pentru magazine online.**
- **Vanzari prin conversatii, fara preturi inventate.**
- **Un asistent de vanzari pe website si WhatsApp, ancorat in catalogul tau real.**

### FAQ Draft

#### What is Nativx Assistant?

Nativx Assistant is an AI sales assistant for online stores. It recommends products, answers shopper questions and helps customers move toward checkout on your website and WhatsApp, with Telegram available for testing.

#### Is it just a chatbot?

No. A generic chatbot answers messages. Nativx Assistant is built around your catalog, products, prices, policies and sales flows. It is designed to recommend, validate and sell, not just chat.

#### Can it invent prices?

No. The assistant checks prices, products and links against your real catalog before sending. If something cannot be verified, it should ask a clarifying question or hand off instead of guessing.

#### Do we need developers?

The service is managed. Nativx handles setup, catalog ingestion, channel connection and testing. Any technical work depends on your store platform and data access, but the product should be sold as done-for-you, not self-serve DIY.

#### Which channels are production-ready?

Website and WhatsApp are the primary commercial channels. Telegram should be described as available for testing until it is hardened for production.

#### Does it replace our support team?

No. It handles repetitive sales and product conversations, then hands off when a human is needed.

#### How is pricing structured?

Pricing should be scoped per store, based on setup complexity, catalog size, channels and monthly conversation volume. The recommended model is a one-time setup fee plus a monthly retainer.

## 10. Demo Script

### Demo goal

In 5 minutes, the prospect must understand:
- assistantul recomanda produse
- raspunde contextual
- nu inventeaza preturi
- poate continua pe canal
- este gestionat de Nativx

### Demo flow

1. Start on website widget

User:
`I'm looking for a moisturizer for dry skin.`

Assistant:
`Here are a few rich moisturizers for dry skin - gentle, fragrance-free formulas that work for sensitive skin too.`

Show:
- 2-3 product cards
- price
- delivery
- rating
- "Sample catalog" label

2. Follow-up

User:
`Something cheaper.`

Assistant:
`Found gentler picks at a lower price - good hydration, simple formulas, still fine for sensitive skin.`

Show:
- revised recommendations
- lower prices
- no fake discount

3. Catalog accuracy moment

Say:
`The important part is not just the answer. It is the check before the answer. Prices and links come from your catalog, not from the model's imagination.`

4. WhatsApp continuation

Show:
- same customer
- same context
- shorter mobile answer
- product card

5. Handoff

User:
`Can I speak with someone?`

Assistant:
`Of course. I am handing this conversation to the team now.`

Say:
`The assistant does not fight the human agent. It stops when the team takes over.`

6. Close

Say:
`For your store, we would start with a private test using your catalog. You approve the behavior before customers see it.`

`Nu pot verifica acest detaliu in catalog acum, asa ca nu vreau sa ghicesc. Iti pot arata alternative disponibile sau pot preda conversatia catre echipa.`

## 13. Pricing & Packaging

### Model recomandat

Nativx Assistant trebuie vandut ca serviciu managed:
- setup fee
- monthly retainer
- optional volume tier / overage

### Ce include setup-ul

- discovery call
- catalog ingestion
- FAQ / policies ingestion
- tone of voice tuning
- website widget setup
- WhatsApp setup, daca datele si contul sunt pregatite
- private test
- approval before launch

### Ce include lunar

- hosting / operation
- tuning
- monitoring
- issue fixes
- updates pe catalog / reguli, in functie de contract
- raportare
- support

### Pachete posibile

#### Starter Pilot

Pentru magazine care vor sa testeze pe un canal si catalog limitat.

Include:
- website widget sau WhatsApp
- catalog partial
- test privat
- demo flow

#### Growth

Pentru magazine care vor sa ruleze comercial.

Include:
- website + WhatsApp
- catalog complet
- FAQ
- handoff
- raportare lunara

#### Managed Plus

Pentru magazine cu volum mai mare sau nevoi speciale.

Include:
- tuning avansat
- mai multe verticale / branduri
- fluxuri de comenzi
- analytics mai detaliat
- SLA, daca poate fi sustinut

Nota: nu publica preturi exacte pana nu exista cost model stabil. Poti spune:

`Pricing is scoped to your catalog, channels and conversation volume. It includes a one-time setup fee and a monthly managed-service retainer.`

## 14. Trust, Compliance & Guardrails

### Claim-uri sigure

Permis:
- `The assistant identifies as AI.`
- `It can hand off to a human.`
- `It checks products, prices and links against catalog data.`
- `You can test it privately before going live.`
- `It is operated as a managed service.`

Cu verificare inainte:
- `GDPR-ready`
- `EU-hosted`
- `data processed in EU`
- `SLA`
- `native integration with [platform]`
- `works with all ecommerce platforms`

### Regula de onestitate

Nu inventa:
- cifre
- clienti
- testimoniale
- logo-uri
- rezultate
- studii de caz
- timpi de implementare
- certificari

Daca ai nevoie de proof, foloseste mecanisme:
- catalog validation
- private testing
- handoff
- sample data label
- demo conversation

### AI Disclosure

Pe website si chat, utilizatorul trebuie sa inteleaga ca vorbeste cu AI.

Exemplu:

`Nativx Assistant - AI sales assistant`

Status:

`Online`

Tooltip / small text:

`AI assistant. Can hand off to the team when needed.`

## 17. Brand Do / Don't

### Do

- Say `AI sales assistant`.
- Lead with online stores.
- Show real ecommerce scenarios.
- Explain catalog validation.
- Be transparent about managed setup.
- Mark sample data clearly.
- Keep copy direct and specific.

### Don't

- Do not call it a chatbot.
- Do not invent metrics.
- Do not use fake testimonials.
- Do not imply native integrations that do not exist.
- Do not present Telegram as production-hardened until it is.
- Do not overuse AI sparkle visuals.
- Do not make the website feel like a generic SaaS template.

## 18. Final Brand Direction

Nativx Assistant should feel like a reliable sales operator, not an AI toy.

The strongest brand idea is:

**A conversation that sells, grounded in the store's real catalog.**

Everything should support that:
- the logo combines chat and upward sales movement
- the hero says AI sales assistant for online stores
- the demo shows products and prices
- the trust story says no invented prices
- the pricing says managed service
- the voice stays plain-spoken and specific

If a new asset, section or message does not reinforce one of these ideas, it should be simplified or removed.
